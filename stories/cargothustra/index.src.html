Thus Spake Cargothustra

The Rise of Nanda

Nanda is the cargo. Every need that arises, and every delivery that answers it, is a step in a random walk through a forest, and iteration slowly makes the walk less random. When East India Gora, whose state-backed pirates call themselves a navy, blockades the trade routes, Vivekananda answers with a swarm of small vessels and a lie.

Play the walk

You are the hand on the table. Walk Nanda from S01 to S10 across the thirteen Cs, and decide the fate of Nanda, Vee, the consignor and the consignee. Each turn you choose an approach (saam, daam, dand or bhed) and one call. Then three Markov chains move: your position on the track, the world (markets high or low, war or peace) and the weather. Keep every one of the thirteen Cs above zero. Same seed and same choices give the same walk.

The outline

Thirteen chapters, one for each C of the cargo model, from Commerce to Corruption, with four mythic interludes between. Pick a chapter or read the whole outline. The bar at the foot of each chapter shows the spread of the cargo's random walk. It narrows as the book iterates, jumps back up at the loss in chapter 10, and closes almost to a line.
