The problem

Heavy cargo moves on documents. It fails on the road.

  • Route surveys and load plans sit in files; permits are PDFs; e-way bills, vehicle and driver records live on separate portals.
  • Road obstacles live in WhatsApp groups and are forgotten after one trip.
  • A route that no longer matches reality is found only at a stoppage or a damage.
India adds its own randomness

A wedding mandap until 1 am. Fresh excavation by a utility crew. Ganpati in Mumbai, Navratri in Kandla, elections in Chennai. For ODC, every halt means crane and escort standby, and disputed claims, because nobody can show what was planned, done and seen.

Source: team concept notes2
The answer

Two portals, one movement record

① ODC.ai · Say what you do

Freight discovery: the shortest route the cargo can actually pass, matched assets, lift-shift, quotation, and buy options for route survey, tracking, insurance and inspection.

② iCargoFinsure · Do what you say

Movement assurance: documents and equipment verified, load-out inspected, journey tracked, escort hurdles handled, unloading supervised, evidence signed.

ODC.ai decides which feasible options to present and procure. iCargoFinsure proves the selected operation ran within its reviewed conditions.

Say what you do

Feasibility is a hard constraint. Distance is the objective.

Shortest road

Often not surveyed: it cannot be planned yet.

Shortest surveyed

May pass under a structure lower than the loaded height.

Recommended

The shortest surveyed route with no red point for this cargo on this trailer.

Survey says pass, numbers say no. When a surveyor writes “normal pass” at a 5.5 m bridge and the cargo stands 5.7 m on its trailer, the twin shows the conflict before the convoy leaves. It is never smoothed away.

Do what you say

The road remembers

The escort master speaks: “Shaadi ka mandap, poora road band hai 1 baje tak.”

The app proposes: event · red · clears 01:00 · hold at the last safe lay-by, pinned to the vehicle’s position. Operations confirms. The hurdle stays on the corridor with its time, and the next movement plans around it.

N-dimensional Markov state

Route-survey obstacles and live, escort-reported obstacles form one state machine per corridor. Festival and monsoon patterns become visible; segments are re-rated Green / Yellow / Red in near real time.

6
The twin

Six layers on one identity, created before the cargo moves

Records

GST, e-way bill (O/R), Vahan, Sarathi, FASTag, MoRTH permit

Route survey

Dynamic, GIS-rich, improves with every passage

Engineering

Dimensions, CG, axle loading, securing, lift plan

Inspection

Measured at load-out; equipment as documented or better

Live tracking

AIS 140 position vs permit and surveyed route

Escort master

Live hurdles, clear instructions, kept for the next movement

No change of state without a confirmed event or an explicit “not observed”.

Ecosystem ask

Three data connections, from MoRTH through NLDSL / ULIP

  1. AIS 140 device data for requested vehicles and prime movers
  2. The detailed JSON of the MoRTH ODC / OWC permit report as an API endpoint
  3. An O (ODC) / R (Regular) toggle on the e-way bill, not available through ULIP today

Asked by 1 January, for National Road Safety Month. We want MoRTH, Customs and DGFT as partners.
